06 — Gravity, not symmetry

The old map distributed nodes on a mirrored spine — which read as machine-generated. The rebuild seeds an intentional composition: foundation elements sit as heavy masses near a center of gravity; character and transformation are held in tension around them; unresolved questions drift lighter at the edges. Then you move things, and your arrangement persists.
